# Trotter domain context

- **Trotter shape** — six-axis internal race classification: class, surface-distance, pace, experience, connections, and volatility.
- **DRF race-shape symbol** — DRF's own pace annotation; not the Trotter shape.
- **Beyer band** — inclusive interval from the highest eligible visible Beyer down ten points.
- **Strong-work flag** — at least one workout with `rank / total <= .20`; descriptive until it beats field prevalence in audited samples.
- **Primary win** — the horse assigned the highest win probability before price.
- **Alternative/value** — a second win path or price-sensitive alternative; not automatically the safest show horse.
- **Safest show** — the highest estimated top-three probability, not a projected third-place finisher.
- **Lock** — a pre-race artifact whose content cannot be changed after results; corrections and reviews are separate additions.
- **Reset condition** — a scratch, surface move, or other published condition that invalidates the prior shape and requires a rebuild.
